*ISOMETRIC VIEW*
The end effector is a dual roller system that is able to nest the pins properly.
The rollers are a hybrid mix of flex wheels and rubber band rollers. 
We've found that this set up in the past has a natural funneling effect on most plastic based game elements.

*TOP VIEW*
This end effector alone is powered by one 5.5W motor and will be also placed on a wrist that is also 5.5W.
The rollers spin reverse of each other in order to intake a full pinnacle smoothly.


*TOP VIEW OF POLYCARBONATE*
We decided to use custom polycarbonate to hardstop the pinnacle when full nestled into the rollers.
The diameter of the hole in the center of the plate is what lets us control how deep the pinnacle can be nested in between the two rollers.
