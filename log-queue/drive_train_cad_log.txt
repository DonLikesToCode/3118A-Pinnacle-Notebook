DRIVETRAIN DESIGN

We decided to limit the chassis to only using 44W worth of motors.
Given the 99W limit, we saw that giving ourselves 55W to hand over to subsystems would be extremely beneficial.
It's also important to mention that in past games, 66W drives were almost mandatory to be competitive.
However, we believe 44W this season is sufficient given that most teams should be mostly
focusing on cycling their match loads.

*ISO VIEW*
The overall drivetrain is 15.5" long and 13.5" wide.
It uses 2.75" wheels with 600 RPM motors to create a 450 RPM ratio.
This ratio is a very acceleration heavy ratio designed to make our cycle times faster.
There will be very little times where we reach top speed on this field.

*TOP VIEW*
Each side has 3 wheels with intentional space left to place the main cross brace. 
There are three braces to hold this drivetrain together, a main cross brace and two intermediate braces that only bind the two inner c-channels together.
Because this drive is only 5 holes wide, a singular cross brace is enough to maintain structural integrity, and the intermidate braces are good enough to prevent bowing.

*SCREW JOINT DIAGRAM*
Screw joints were used to lower friction as shown. Screw joints has less slop than shafts and we want to use them almost everywhere (when feasible).
We used brass inserts, as they have the lowest amount of slop and friction when spinning on the screws.
The screw is mounted a cantilever to the inner c-channel and then supported by the mounted bearing flat on the outer c-channel.
